# Shifa Brand

## When to use
- redesigning any page
- choosing typography, colors, or imagery
- writing or editing copy tone
- deciding whether a visual element fits the brand
- reviewing whether a change feels like Shifa Al Quran

## Brand identity
- Bengali-first content presentation; strong Bengali typography.
- Arabic Quran text must remain crisp, readable, and never faux-bolded.
- Respectful Islamic visual treatment; no stereotypical motifs.
- Calm, trustworthy, spiritually respectful tone.
- Professional but warm; not corporate, not generic SaaS.
- Modern and refined; not futuristic, not decorative.

## Approved visual language
- Deep ink backgrounds where appropriate.
- Warm parchment/light surfaces.
- Emerald/teal for meaningful actions.
- Restrained gold for spiritual or ornamental accents.
- Generous whitespace.
- Refined card proportions.
- Subtle borders and controlled elevation.
- Geometric Islamic influence only when appropriate and restrained.

## Prohibited patterns
- Generic SaaS gradients or glassmorphism.
- Blue/purple startup landing-page aesthetics.
- Excessive rounded cards everywhere.
- Excessive floating UI or decorative icons.
- Random animations or heavy motion.
- Visual clutter.
- Fake statistics, fake testimonials, invented Quranic verses, or unsupported medical claims.

## Rules
- Use existing design tokens; do not hardcode colors.
- Do not alter Quran text or religious content without explicit instruction.
- Do not make guaranteed healing claims; healing is by the will of Allah.
- Every page must feel like part of the same website, not a copied template.
- The homepage is already redesigned; refine it rather than rebuilding.
- Other pages should receive page-specific compositions, not cloned homepage layouts.
